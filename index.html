Privacy Policy

Product: YouTube Transcript Downloader & Search (Chrome extension)
Effective date: 2026-05-18
Contact: mishasworlds@gmail.com

Summary

YouTube Transcript Downloader & Search is a Chrome extension that lets you view, search, copy, and export the caption text of YouTube videos. The extension runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate a server. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

What the extension accesses

DataWhyWhere it goes
Caption (timedtext) data from the current YouTube watch page To display the transcript, allow search and timestamp navigation, and export TXT / SRT / VTT files Stays in your browser. Read directly from YouTube on the page you opened; never sent to us.
Your preferences (last selected caption language, view mode, panel state) To restore your settings on the next video Stored locally via Chrome's storage API. Never leaves your browser unless you have Chrome sync enabled (in which case Google syncs it to your other Chrome installs under your account).

What the extension does NOT do

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or tracking pixels.
  • No advertising; no ad networks.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No remote code execution. All JavaScript is bundled at build time from local sources.
  • No transmission of transcript text, video titles, watch history, or any other content to us or to third parties.
  • No AI or external API calls in this version.

Permissions used

  • sidePanel — to render the transcript reader UI in Chrome's side panel.
  • storage — to persist your local preferences (described above).
  • Host permission https://www.youtube.com/* — to read the public caption data on YouTube watch pages.

We request only the permissions strictly required to deliver the feature you installed the extension for.

Children

The extension is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Third-party services

The extension communicates only with YouTube (https://www.youtube.com/*) and only to read caption data on the page you are already viewing. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to your use of YouTube itself: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the extension handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the extension's Chrome Web Store changelog.
